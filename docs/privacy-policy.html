Cast Anything — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 July 2026 · applies to the Cast Anything Android app (ai.jeram.castanything).

Short version: Cast Anything does not have accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and does not collect, store or sell your personal data. It runs on your local Wi‑Fi network and talks directly to your TV.

What the app accesses on your device

  • Photos, videos & audio you choose. When you pick a file (or a folder) to cast, the app reads it only to stream it to your selected TV and, if you ask, to convert it to a TV‑friendly format. These files stay on your device and on your local network — they are never uploaded to us or to any server we control.
  • Converted copies are created on your device. They are kept in the app's cache for the current cast, or saved to your gallery only if you explicitly choose to.

Network access

  • Your local network. The app discovers TVs/media players on your Wi‑Fi and serves the chosen file from your phone to the TV. This traffic stays on your local network.
  • Media links you enter. If you paste a web URL or live stream, the app fetches it so it can be played on your TV.
  • Subtitle search (optional). If you search for subtitles, the app sends the title you are searching for and your chosen language to subdl.com to find matching subtitle files, and downloads the one you pick. This happens only when you use that feature. See SUBDL's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash‑reporting SDKs, and no tracking. We do not receive any personal information from the app.

Permissions and why they're used

  • Media / storage — to let you pick the videos, music and photos you want to cast.
  • Internet & network/Wi‑Fi state, multicast — to discover TVs on your network, serve files to them, and fetch any media link you enter.
  • Notifications & foreground service (media playback) — to show playback controls and keep casting working while your screen is off.

Children

Cast Anything is a general‑purpose utility and is not directed at children.
