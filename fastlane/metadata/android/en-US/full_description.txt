Cast Anything turns your phone into a remote for your TV. Pick a video, song, or photo — or paste a link — and it plays on your Samsung TV, or any DLNA-compatible TV or media player, on the same Wi-Fi. No cables, no extra hardware.

WHAT YOU CAN CAST
• Videos, music and photos from your phone
• Media links — paste any http/https URL or an .m3u8 live stream
• A whole folder at once, as a playlist

A REAL REMOTE IN YOUR POCKET
• Play, pause, stop, skip and seek
• Volume control
• Controls on your lock screen and in a notification — keep playing with the screen off
• A live progress bar, so you always know where you are

PLAYS FILES YOUR TV NORMALLY CAN'T
• A built-in converter re-encodes HEVC, MKV and 4K on your device into a format your TV can play
• Pick speed vs. quality, and optionally save the converted copy to your gallery

SUBTITLES
• Add your own .srt file, or search thousands of subtitles online in 7 languages

QUEUE & PLAYLISTS
• Line up a whole evening — shuffle, repeat and auto-play across the room
• Add an entire folder in one tap

MAKE IT YOURS
• 16 accent colors, plus light, dark and AMOLED themes
• Swappable app icons

GOOD TO KNOW (please read)
Cast Anything uses DLNA, the casting standard that Samsung and many other TVs support. That means:
• It streams files and media links — it does NOT mirror your whole screen. For screen mirroring, use your phone's built-in Smart View / Screen Mirroring.
• It can't relay DRM-protected apps like Netflix, YouTube or Disney+ — those use their own casting.
• Your TV still needs to support the file's format (H.264 MP4 plays almost everywhere; the built-in converter helps with the rest).
• Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Cast Anything is lightweight, ad-free and account-free — it talks directly to your TV on your own network. Just scan, pick, and cast.
